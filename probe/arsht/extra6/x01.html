Adrienne Arsht Center for the Performing Arts of Miami-Dade County

Arts & Culture

The Arsht proudly serves as the cultural pulse of Miami – the heart of magical live arts experiences that spark the imagination and connect people to one another. Whether on our stages or in your neighborhood, the Arsht is alive year-round with international artists, innovative programming from resident companies and local arts partners, free community events that reflect Miami’s unique identity and more than 100 culturally diverse and impactful learning experiences for 80,000 children every year.

Since opening in 2006 in the heart of downtown, the Arsht, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, has been recognized as a leader in the city’s cultural transformation, a catalyst for billions of dollars in new development and a host venue for historic events. The 300+ annual Arsht events include a robust series of touring Broadway musicals direct from New York, star-studded jazz and classical music concerts curated for South Florida, a major annual Flamenco Festival and an award-winning Miami-based theater program. In addition, Family Fest, Gospel Fest Miami, Art + Mind Day, Heritage Fest and our LGBTQ+ Pride celebration are among dozens of free events that bring people from all corners of our community together. For more information, visit arshtcenter.org.

The Arsht Offers an Exceptional Dining Experience

Grab a seat and indulge in the ambiance. The Arsht is thrilled to offer preshow light bites and dining options at select shows.

  • Teatro Restaurant: Located on the second floor of the Ziff Ballet Opera House, guests can enjoy a two-course, prix fixe menu two and a half hours before select performances. Preshow dining reservations are encouraged.
  • Accent Café: Located outside the Ziff Ballet Opera House, guests can partake in creative cocktails, a hot beverage station, beer and wine as well as freshly baked pastries, empanadas, snacks and treats.

For more information or to make a reservation, visit ArshtCenter.org/Dining.

The Arsht also offers a variety of spaces that can be customized to fit almost any type of live theatrical or filmed event, including plays, concerts, weddings, live broadcasts, award shows, commercials, album release parties, and more. Across its sprawling, two-block, 525,000-square-foot campus, the Arsht can accommodate almost any type of event from small meetings to large scale events.

The Arsht campus includes:

  • Ziff Ballet Opera House: 2,400 seats.
  • John S. and James L. Knight Concert Hall: 2,200 seats.
  • Carnival Studio Theater: A flexible 70’x70’ black-box space designed for up to 200 seats.
  • Parker and Vann Thomson Plaza for the Arts: An outdoor social and performance space linking the two main houses across Biscayne Boulevard, and home to Live on the Plaza.
  • Peacock Foundation Studio: A rehearsal room and event space that can accommodate up to 300 guests.
  • Peacock Education Center: A 3,500-square-foot workshop and classroom space.
  • Teatro: A full-service, 88-seat restaurant that is open to the public before performances and available to rent on non-performance days.

Every customized event is handled by experienced on-site industry professionals working with top-of-the-line, state-of-the-art equipment and the best vendors Miami has to offer, ensuring that every occasion is flawless and unforgettable. No matter how big or how small, the Arsht can turn your vision into a reality.
