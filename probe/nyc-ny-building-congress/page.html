Event FAQs

How can I register for upcoming events?

Registration for all events is accessible on the website. Each event will have a link for registration or relevant contact information. Pre-payment is required for all events. For questions regarding events, contact Logan Ellis, Director, Events.

What types of events does the New York Building Congress host?

The New York Building Congress hosts a variety of events designed to foster networking, professional development, and industry collaboration. These events include breakfast forums, a gala, luncheons, committee meetings, a golf outing and more.

Can non-members attend events?

Non-members can attend all events, but committee and council meetings are for members only.

Where can I find the event calendar?

A list of all confirmed events is on the website under the events tab.
