Event Information

The next meeting of the Council on Innovation & Best Practices will take place on Thursday, October 29 at the New York Building Congress. Breakfast will be available at 8:30 AM and the meeting will begin at 8:45 AM.

This session will feature the second installment of our interactive “AI Clinic” series, where a select group of AI-enabled construction startups will deliver brief presentations highlighting real-world applications of artificial intelligence on the jobsite. Presentations will focus on presenters’ own technologies, from construction payroll to jobsite data collection and analysis tools.

The format is designed to foster a candid, peer-to-peer exchange, giving members the opportunity to learn directly from firms developing innovative technologies across the industry.

Committee meetings are free and open to members of the Building Congress but you must be registered to attend. To register, please contact our Membership Team.
