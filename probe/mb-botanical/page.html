Pilates in the Garden (Tuesdays)

  • Tuesday, November 3, 2026
  • 9:30 PM 10:30 PM
  • Google Calendar ICS

Step away from the everyday and reconnect with your body in the beautiful surroundings of the Miami Beach Botanical Garden.

Join The Pilates Place Studios for a rejuvenating Classical Mat Pilates class led by Victoria Collins, rooted in the original method developed by Joseph Pilates. This full-body practice combines mindful movement, focused breathing, core strength, mobility, and control to leave you feeling stronger, longer, and more connected to your body.

Whether you are new to Pilates or have been practicing for years, this class offers an opportunity to experience the timeless Pilates method in a relaxed, inspiring outdoor setting.

Expect a thoughtfully guided mat class that honors the classical sequence while making the practice accessible and enjoyable for all levels. Come move, breathe, strengthen, and reset surrounded by nature.

What to bring: A Pilates or yoga mat, water, and comfortable clothing.

No previous Pilates experience necessary.

Come for the movement. Stay for the feeling. 🌿

About Victoria Collins:

Victoria is a Miami-based Pilates instructor with a background in education and a passion for mind-body alignment through intentional movement and breathwork. Victoria discovered mat Pilates at age 16 during a time when she was navigating her own mental health and physical wellness challenges. She immediately fell in love with the method as a powerful tool to regain control of her body and calm her mind. Years later, her sister-in-law introduced her to Pilates classes using the reformer at The Pilates Place Studios. This introduction reignited that initial spark, inspiring her to complete her Pilates teacher training at The Pilates Place Studios so she could help others experience the transformative power of the practice.

Victoria firmly believes that Pilates is for every body—regardless of age, background, or fitness level. Her classes combine classical and contemporary techniques into a challenging, full-body workout powered by the breath. Expect a warm, supportive, and encouraging environment where you can build mobility, strength, balance, and mind-body awareness.

In the Lab: The Little Wetland

  • Saturday, November 7, 2026
  • 10:00 AM 11:30 AM
  • Google Calendar ICS

The ecological intimacy of a swamp, none of the physical inconveniences. In the Lab: The Little Wetland brings children face-to-face with living swamp flora, including aquatic carnivorous plants. Children study the plants in individual aquariums, then come together to create a miniature living wetland in a shallow vessel.


About Sasha Sikaffy:

Sasha S. Sikaffy, M.S., is an environmental scientist and educator informed by years spent in coral reef fieldwork and marine science education.

Her work considers how children come to know the living world through direct ecological encounter—attention to living systems, intimacy with place, and sustained contact with the more-than-human world.

In The Lab is her ongoing study of South Florida ecology, in which children engage with living organisms, biological specimens, and scientific instruments across field and lab settings.


Important Information:

Designed for children ages 4-8, with one accompanying adult per child.

All materials and scientific tools are provided.

Comfortable clothing suitable for working with water is recommended, along with a water flask.

Nature. In Community

  • Sunday, November 15, 2026
  • 1:00 PM 4:00 PM
  • Google Calendar ICS

On Sunday, November 15, the Miami Beach Botanical Garden transforms into an open landscape of participatory installations—a living laboratory of the senses.

Move freely through interactive stations across the garden: weave, touch, smell, listen, and contribute to an evolving collective creation.

The Experience

  • Participatory Installations: Engage with natural fibers, botanical scents, tactile native elements, and acoustic soundscapes.

  • Collective Making: Contribute your individual gestures of writing, weaving, and reflection into shared works that evolve in real time.

  • Guest Artists: Featuring creative collaborations with guest artists Anna Biondo and Sarah Laing.

The Vision

Created by neuroscientist and artist Karina Del Punta, Ph.D., Nature In Community draws from insights in environmental neuroscience and relational ecology. The work explores what it means to meet the natural world in active reciprocity—rediscovering ourselves not just as visitors or consumers, but as vital participants woven into the fabric of the living landscape.

Through the intentional orchestration of materials, colors, sounds, and scents, the experience invites a state of soft fascination—a restorative mode of attention that calms the nervous system while heightening relational and creative presence. Each individual reflection shared throughout the afternoon weaves into a tangible representation of community and ecological interdependence.

Discover what takes shape when we create together.

Rooted in Gratitude: A Sound Bath and Breathwork Journey

  • Tuesday, November 17, 2026
  • 6:30 PM 7:45 PM
  • Google Calendar ICS

A breathwork & sound journey into the alchemical power of embodied gratitude.

This 75-minute journey opens seated, beginning with yogic breathing practices that prepare the body and invite presence. From there, you'll move into an active, alchemizing breathwork experience done lying down, moving what feels stagnant, returning you to your essential Self, and attuning you to the wellspring of ease and joy within. The session closes with an immersive sound journey that supports integration and reestablishes inner harmony.

In this season of gratitude, rather than a practice that asks us to overlook our struggles, we expand our awareness to hold difficulty and recognize what is already supportive, already whole, already here — embodying gratitude as the alchemical emotion it truly is.

Walk away feeling lighter, more spacious, and more connected to an inner refuge.

Rooted in ancient wisdom traditions and informed by modern research.

What to Bring:

Feel free to bring a light covering and anything that helps you feel completely at ease—an extra pillow under your head, an eye cover for a deeper experience, or a mat. The more comfortable, the better.

Before You Arrive:

Doors to the Banyan Ballroom close sharply at 6:40 pm so as not to interrupt the experience. We encourage you to arrive early and take in the beauty of the garden — bring your journal or walk the grounds to ground yourself before we begin.

About Franci Blanco:

Franci is a Miami-based wellness facilitator and coach with roughly ten years of experience teaching yoga, breathwork, sound bath, and meditation. She completed her yoga teacher training in Rishikesh, India, and has returned three times since for month-long periods of continued study. She has also completed three ten-day Vipassana meditation retreats and her first year of professional training in the application of therapeutic sound through The Center of Light Institute of Sound Healing and Shamanic Studies. Franci teaches across venues including The Standard and Miami Beach Botanical Garden, and is currently pursuing her Master of Social Work. Her classes are rooted in breath awareness and nervous system support, inviting a deeper connection to the body's own wisdom.
