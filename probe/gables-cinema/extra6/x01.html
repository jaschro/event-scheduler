Coral Gables Art Cinema

Arts & Culture

In its 12th year, the Coral Gables Art Cinema continues to serve South Florida as its premier nonprofit cinema with a mission to bring the best independent, foreign, and classic films to the region's vibrant, diverse, multicultural and multilingual community. Open 365 days a year, its state-of-the-art auditorium boasts 141 seats, 4K digital projection, 3D, and often shows films on 35mm & 70mm. The cinema's National Theatre Live program is a groundbreaking project broadcasting the best of British theatre from the London stage to the cinema at a fraction of the cost of a stage play. Dedicated to educating the next generation of cinema goers, the cinema produces the Miami Children's Film Festival, a culturally rich and entertaining annual event. The monthly Family Day on Aragon programming brings classic family friendly film titles in the form of inexpensive family fun to the community. The Coral Gables Art Cinema is truly where great films live.

One of the Southeastern U.S.'s leading art house venues, this 141-seat theater located in a jewel of a Mediterranean-style building showcases premieres of international and American cinema, classic films, special events, visiting filmmakers and more.
