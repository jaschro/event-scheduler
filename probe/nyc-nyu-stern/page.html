— November 5, 2026

The AI Playbook Series: Bart Consedine, Head of AI Engineering at Magnite

The AI Playbook is a virtual Fireside Chat series featuring senior executives from across industries sharing how they are responding to, and building with, artificial intelligence. Moderated by Fubon Innovation Director Melissa Schilling, each conversation moves beyond headlines and hype to explore the practical decisions leaders are making: where they are testing AI, how they are managing risk and change, what has worked, and what they would do differently.

Join us on November 5th at 6PM EST for a conversation with Bart Consedine, Head of AI Engineering at Magnite.
