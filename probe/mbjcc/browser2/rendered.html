Something for Everyone, All Year Long

The MBJCC is a hub of community life in Miami Beach, hosting events throughout the year that bring people together — from Jewish holiday celebrations and author talks to games, lectures, and arts and culture programming. Whether you are a member or a guest, there is always something happening at the J worth showing up for. Our events span all ages and interests, with recurring programs like Wednesdays at the J, Bridge, literary events, and special community celebrations on the Jewish calendar. Browse our upcoming events in the section below, or explore event categories to find what interests you. Sign up for our newsletter to stay in the loop on everything happening at the MBJCC.

Events FAQs

Most MBJCC events are open to members and the broader community. Some events may be member-only or have limited capacity — check individual event listings for access details and any membership requirements.

Some events are free and walk-in, while others require advance registration or an RSVP. Individual event listings will indicate whether registration is required and provide ticketing or sign-up links.

Sign up for the MBJCC newsletter to receive regular updates on upcoming events, programs, and community news. You can also follow us on Facebook and Instagram for the latest listings.

The MBJCC hosts a wide variety of events including Jewish holiday commemorations and celebrations, the Wednesdays at the J weekly series, Lunch & Learn talks, literary and author events, arts and culture programming, games and social recreation, community screenings, and more.
