Antisemitism and Free Speech in America, Past and Present

Outlook

How has antisemitism shaped American life, and what makes this moment different? Join Hartman Senior Fellow at the Kogod Research Center, historian James Loeffler, for a conversation about antisemitism, free speech and the challenges facing American democracy. Drawing on Loeffler’s new book, Exceptional Hatred: Antisemitism and the Fight over Free Speech in Modern America, the evening will explore why Jews and Israel so often stand at the center of debates over free expression, and how we can confront hatred while protecting fundamental freedoms. We will consider what history reveals about the fragility and resilience of liberal democracy, and where we might go from here.

Join us for an evening of conversation with esteemed scholar and author James Loeffler as he discusses his new book Exceptional Hatred: Antisemitism and the Fight Over Free Speech in Modern America.
