Equality and the City: Bogotá’s Sustainable Urbanism

Event Global Exchange
December 8th, 2026
6:00pm - 8:00pm
Gensler
1700 Broadway, New York, NY, USA

Enrique Peñalosa is an internationally respected urban strategy and leadership consultant and lecturer who, as Mayor of Bogotá in two non-consecutive terms, profoundly transformed one of Latin America’s largest cities. His innovative work as mayor — including the creation of a world-class bus-based mass transit system, a pioneering and extensive cycling infrastructure, more than a hundred kilometers of greenways through a very dense city, housing projects for hundreds of thousands, and the generation of parks all through the city among many more things — as well as his lecturing and consulting work has influenced policies in hundreds of cities across the globe. Peñalosa is the author of Equality and the City.

Join us for a conversation with Enrique Peñalosa, former Mayor of Bogotá, Colombia, about the city’s sustainable transit network, Transmilenio, and his administration’s achievements in the public realm.

Secured by major investment during Peñalosa’s two mayoral terms, Bogotá’s Transmilenio has become a global model for bus rapid transit (BRT) systems. As mayor, Peñalosa expanded the city’s Ciclovía program, closing 75 miles of major roads to cars every Sunday. His administration prioritized connecting Bogotá’s outskirts to the city center, and he oversaw the construction of more than 300 miles of bike lanes during his terms in office.

What bold steps can improve access to transportation and public space for all city dwellers?

About Global Exchange

Global Exchange connects New York City’s civic community to the world’s foremost urban leaders and inspires creative change around shared challenges, from housing and infrastructure to health and resilience. The Global Exchange Speaker Series brings experts from around the world to New York City for lectures and conversations with an audience of distinguished leaders across the fields of policy, advocacy, design, philanthropy, planning and law.
